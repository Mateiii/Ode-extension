# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Academic researchers (students, postgrads, dissertation writers) and knowledge workers (journalists, analysts, consultants) doing serious desk research. They use Øde in active reading sessions — browser open, source in one tab, side panel alongside. They are focused, often under time pressure, and treating the tool as infrastructure: it should disappear into the workflow, not demand attention.

## Product Purpose

Øde is a research co-pilot that lives in the Chrome side panel. It lets users ask AI questions grounded in the active page or an uploaded document, save and organise research notes with citations, fact-check claims, and build a bibliography — without leaving the tab they're reading. Success: the researcher spends less time managing information and more time thinking about it.

## Brand Personality

Calm, scholarly, precise.

Øde feels like a well-maintained research desk: ordered, quiet, capable. It earns trust through consistency and density — not through decoration or enthusiasm. It doesn't celebrate itself. It works.

Voice: direct, minimal, no filler. Labels are verbs. Confirmations are short. Error messages state the fact, then the action.

## Anti-references

- **AI chat products (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude.ai):** dark sidebar, white bubble chat, glowing blue accent, floating input. Øde should not read as "another AI chat box."
- **Generic SaaS (Notion-adjacent):** cream backgrounds, rounded-everything, San Francisco or DM Sans, pastel empty-state illustrations, soft shadows. Comfortable but forgettable.
- **Browser extension defaults:** flat grey panels, system fonts, pure Tailwind slate — zero identity. The current state of Øde.

## Design Principles

1. **Content is the protagonist.** Research content — notes, citations, AI responses — fills the viewport. Panel chrome (headers, tabs, toolbars) is minimal and receding. If an element isn't carrying information, it doesn't carry space.

2. **Earned restraint.** Every design decision must pay for itself. No decorative borders, gradient text, ambient glows, or illustration. When something looks good here, it's because it's correct — not because it's styled.

3. **Scholarly confidence.** Øde operates in an academic register. Typography and spacing signal precision and care. It should feel like a tool built by someone who reads — not by someone who growth-hacks.

4. **Density without crowding.** The panel is 400px wide and full-height. Information must be dense enough to be useful without feeling cramped. Spacing is intentional rhythm, not padding-by-default.

5. **Quiet persistence.** Øde runs alongside the user's real work. Motion is reserved for feedback (saves, loads, transitions between states). Nothing pulses, bounces, or demands attention unprompted.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA target: 4.5:1 body text contrast, 3:1 large/bold text, keyboard-navigable UI, visible focus rings, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected on all transitions and animations.
